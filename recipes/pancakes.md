#Pancakes

##Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 2 cups of milk
- 1.5 cups cups of flour
- 1 tbsp of suger
- Pinch of salt

##Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs and milk together.
2. Addd the flour, sugar and salt, mix until smooth.
3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
4. Pour in a small ladle of batter and cook until golden on both sides.
5. Serve with jam or berries.
